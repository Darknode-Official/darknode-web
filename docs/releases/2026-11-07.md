## Security / rules / data changes
- Worker: new route POST /api/v1/usage (signed in), in worker/usage.js.
  - Products are nexus and app. It stores version, OS, first and last seen and ping count, plus app VM counts (count, running, last start).
  - Writes go to usage/{uid} with FIREBASE_SA. A repeat ping within 10 minutes writes nothing.
- Rules: usage/{uid} can be read by the owner only and is never writable from a client.
- Clients send at most every 6 hours and only when signed in. Off with Nexus `/shareusage off`, NEXUS_SHARE_USAGE=0 or DO_NOT_TRACK=1, or the app's Settings toggle.
