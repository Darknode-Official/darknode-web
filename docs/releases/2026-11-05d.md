## What changed (user-visible)
- Workspace plan sharing (owner request 2026-11-05). On the Team page, a team workspace owner on Team or above has a "Let members use my plan" toggle.
  - When it is on, members get the owner's plan inside that workspace only: sidebar tools, team sync, monitors and API keys.
  - Personal workspaces and AI chat stay on each person's own plan.
  - Members see a note on the Team and Plans pages. Switching into or out of a sharing workspace rebuilds the Deck for the new plan.
  - The Team plan card lists "Optionally share your plan with workspace members".
## Security / rules / data changes
- Rules: a new owner-only update path on workspaces/{wid} for sharePlan, sharePlanBy and sharePlanTier.
  - sharePlanBy must be the caller.
  - sharePlanTier must be the caller's real plan: the admin claim counts as enterprise, otherwise users/{uid}.tier.
- Worker: new worker/plan-share.js. It is used by /api/graph, /api/monitor and /api/keys on team workspaces.
  - It ignores the tier stored on the workspace doc.
  - It checks that the sharer is still the owner, using the caller's token.
  - It reads the sharer's live users/{uid} tier and admin fields with FIREBASE_SA.
  - Results are cached for 60s per workspace. It fails closed to the caller's own plan.
