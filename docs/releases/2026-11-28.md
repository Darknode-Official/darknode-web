## What changed (user-visible)
- Two new monitor types, both part of the Free plan's one-domain watch:
  - **Domain registration** (`domain-expiry`) reads the registry's RDAP record, found through the IANA bootstrap. It alerts when:
    - the registration expires within 30 days (adjustable);
    - the registration has lapsed, or the domain is not registered;
    - a transfer to another registrar is pending;
    - the transfer lock is off (gTLDs only).

    A subdomain is checked by its registrable domain.
  - **Website** (`web-posture`) checks:
    - HTTPS and the HTTP redirect;
    - HSTS, CSP, clickjacking, nosniff and cookie flags (graded by the posture engine);
    - an exposed `/.git/HEAD` or `/.env`, recognised from the first 2 KB, contents never stored.
- Once a domain has a baseline, a new or weaker finding from either check alerts as drift, whatever its severity. Email-posture already worked this way.
- "Watch this domain" now sets up four monitors. An existing Free watch shows **Add the other checks** under Monitors.
- Alert emails explain each new finding: what happened, why it matters and how to fix it.
- Copy updated:
  - docs FAQ, Privacy and Changelog;
  - landing pricing card;
  - Plans;
  - JSON-LD;
  - Monitors help.

## Security / rules / data changes
- New outbound requests from the Worker:
  - data.iana.org and the registries' RDAP servers;
  - the watched site's home page over HTTPS and HTTP, plus `/.git/HEAD` and `/.env`.
- Private and local hosts are refused (net-guard `hostAllowed`), including on redirects.
- Stored: findings (title, severity, header detail, the URL of an exposed file) and per-domain baselines. File contents are never stored.
- No TOS_VERSION bump:
  - Section 14 already covers what monitors store.
  - Sections 1 and 11 already require authorisation.
  - The docs FAQ says to watch only sites you own or may check.
- No rules, secrets, bindings or migrations changed. CSP unchanged.
