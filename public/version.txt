DARKNODE OS  (bootable VM image / from-scratch x86 kernel)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current: 1.0.0

1.0.0    2026-09-11   Release tag.
0.1.0    2026-09-11   First tagged kernel.

What currently boots (reproduced, not asserted): the kernel builds and links,
produces a GRUB (multiboot2) rescue ISO, and boots under QEMU
(qemu-system-i386) to a framebuffer GUI. Not yet verified: boot on real
hardware, and on emulators other than QEMU. First commit 2026-09-10.
